# The Backstory

I remember the exact moment the idea for this landed. It wasn't a flash of brilliance, but rather a slow, creeping realization born out of pure frustration. 

I was working on yet another project, juggling ten different tools, trying to make them talk to each other. The friction was exhausting. Every step forward felt like wading through molasses. 

### Why we build this

The truth is, building software shouldn't feel like a battle against your own tools. We started this project not because we wanted to build another product, but because we desperately needed it ourselves. 

We wanted a space where creativity wasn't bogged down by setup, configuration, and endless context switching. A minimalist, powerful environment that just gets out of your way.

### The Journey

It's been a wild ride. Nights turned into mornings. Countless prototypes thrown away. But seeing it finally come together, seeing people use it to build things faster and better—it makes every second worth it.

This is just the beginning.
